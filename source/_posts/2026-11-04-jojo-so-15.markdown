Hello again, and welcome back to the blog! It's time to take a very brief look at Episode #15 for Stone Ocean - "Ultra Security House Unit"!

<br>

If there's one Stone Ocean episode that really didn't need any changes it's certainly this one - even in its broadcast version it looked incredible! Every shot was on model, everything looked amazing - they really went all out for Annasui's introductory episode. But in any case, we still have a couple of extremely minor things to check out, so let's dive in!

<br>
