Stone Ocean #15 - Ultra Security House Unit

Hello again, and welcome back to the blog! It’s time to take a very brief look at Episode #15 for Stone Ocean - “Ultra Security House Unit”!

Every shot was on model, everything looked amazing - they really went all out for Annasui’s introductory episode.
